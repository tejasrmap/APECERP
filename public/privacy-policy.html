2. Location Information & Background Location Tracking

🚨 PROMINENT DISCLOSURE (Google Play User Data Compliance):

The APEC ERP mobile app collects precise location data (GPS, network-based) and background location data even when the app is closed, minimized, or not in foreground use, to enable:

  • Verification of employee presence and duty check-in at assigned client substations, transformers, and power project sites.
  • Live field workforce coordination and dispatching during electrical maintenance operations.
  • Real-time route tracking and historical transit logging during active shifts.
  • Ensuring the occupational safety of electrical engineers and lone-workers in hazardous high-voltage field environments.

Duty-Bound Collection Only: Background location tracking is active strictly during an employee's active duty shift or work schedule. Background tracking stops automatically upon clocking out / ending the shift. Location data is never sold or shared with advertisers.

Specific Location Permissions Used:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION: Accesses high-accuracy GPS coordinates for precise substation geofencing and site verification.
  • ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Accesses cell-tower and Wi-Fi network approximate positioning for battery-efficient initial location detection.
  • ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION: Allows location tracking while the engineer is traveling between power installations or using other apps while on shift.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION: Keeps an active Android foreground notification visible to the user whenever location tracking is active, ensuring 100% transparency.

User Control: You have full control over location services. You can grant or revoke location permissions at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > APEC ERP > Permissions > Location). Please note that location access is mandatory for duty attendance check-in as an employee of APEC Power Solutions.

4. Camera & Storage Access

The App requests access to your device's Camera (android.permission.CAMERA) and file storage.

  • Field Progress Photos: To allow technicians to take on-site photographs of equipment, electrical panels, cables, and meter readings for Daily Work Reports.
  • Profile Image: To allow employees to set or update their profile avatar in the organization directory.

The App does not access your personal photo gallery or camera without your explicit user action (tapping the camera or upload button).

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted enterprise cloud infrastructure providers to operate the application securely:

  • Google Play Services: Used for platform APIs, Google authentication, and device-level location providers.
  • Google Firebase: Used for Firebase Authentication, Firestore real-time database, Firebase Cloud Storage (storing encrypted report photos), and Cloud Messaging for operational alerts.
  • Supabase: Used as a secure cloud database for storing project records, daily logs, and user metadata.

These service providers are contractually bound to safeguard your data and are prohibited from using it for any purpose other than providing infrastructure services for APEC ERP. We do NOT embed third-party advertising SDKs, ad networks, or data-broker trackers.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies, all users have the right to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion:

Employees, former employees, or contractors can initiate an account deletion request by emailing:

admin@apecpowersolutions.com or managingdirector@apecpowersolutions.com

Please specify: (1) Your Full Name, (2) Registered Mobile Phone Number or Work Email, and (3) Subject: "Account Deletion Request - APEC ERP".

What Happens Upon Deletion:

  • Immediately Removed: User login credentials, authentication tokens, profile photos, personal phone numbers, and live location records will be purged from the active database within 30 days.
  • Retained for Legal & Audit Compliance: Historical engineering reports, statutory timesheets, signed daily work orders, and safety logs submitted during your employment will be retained in internal enterprise archives for statutory compliance and accounting audits as required by Indian industrial regulations.

7. Data Security & Storage

We implement rigorous organizational and technical measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the mobile application and cloud servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS 1.3).
  • Encrypted Storage: Sensitive data stored in Google Firebase and Supabase are encrypted at rest.
  • Role-Based Access Control: Only authorized administrative personnel of APEC Power Solutions have access to operational logs and employee records.
